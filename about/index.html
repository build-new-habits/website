Why I built it

I’m Graeme. I was playing football, running and tennis. Then I got injured, and I couldn’t.

My story

Apps told me to push. I couldn’t afford a physio. Walking into a gym took energy I didn’t have.

I needed something that worked around what I could give that day. It didn’t exist, so I built it.

No streaks. No guilt when you’ve been away. A coach that asks how you are first.

What I believe

  • Stopping isn’t failingIf you disappear for two weeks, something was going on. The right answer is a kind welcome back.
  • Everyone deserves good supportSafety and Wellbeing are the same on Free and the Plan. Always.
  • We are not separateMoving is one way we reconnect, with ourselves and with each other.

What I hope it does

Feeling understood changes how you meet a hard day. You come back more easily.

And you might find a little more room for the people around you. They’re having a day too.
