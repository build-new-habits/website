Hello. I’m Graeme, and I built Alongside.

For a long time, I was the person fitness apps were made for. I ran. I played football and tennis. Probably too much, but I loved it.

Then I got injured. My hamstring, my glutes, my lower back. Running stopped. Some days, even walking far was hard.

So I opened fitness apps. The ones I tried didn’t ask whether I was carrying an injury. Or how much energy I had. Or what kind of day it was. They told me to push through. My body was telling me something different.

I went through it in stages. Motivated first. Then frustrated. Then disappointed, in myself as much as in the apps. Then sad. And then I gave up.

But the injury was only part of it. I’ve spent a lot of my life feeling like the rules changed when I wasn’t looking. Feeling judged. Feeling I should just know things nobody had taught me.

What I needed wasn’t a programme. It was something that understood where I was, and worked with what I could give that day.

It didn’t exist. So I built it.

Alongside asks how you are before it asks anything of you. When your energy is low, the session is gentler. When something’s sore, it leaves some moves out. There are no streaks, and no telling you off for the days you didn’t come.

And there’s one more thing I hope it does. Everyone you pass today is carrying a day you can’t see. Once you’ve been met where you are, it gets a little easier to meet other people there too.

I hope you find it useful.
