Put it on your phone

Alongside installs like an app: its own icon, full screen, no browser bar, and it works offline.

  1. Android: open app.buildnewhabits.co.uk in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then Install app.
  2. iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
